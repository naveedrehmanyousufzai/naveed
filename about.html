About

Naveed Rehman Yousufzai picked up a racket at the Karachi Gymkhana at nine years old and has not put it down since. He won his first inter-school title at fifteen, turned professional at nineteen, and has since played PSA events across eleven countries.

Off court he serves as Secretary of the Sindh Squash Association, where he runs the provincial junior circuit and coaches at a club level. Much of his week is spent getting younger players into competitive draws earlier than he managed to.
